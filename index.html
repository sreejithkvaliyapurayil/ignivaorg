Our businesses

Five business verticals

Convention & Trade Centres

IGNIVA Calicut Trade Centre in Kozhikode and IGNIVA Cochin Trade Centre on Willingdon Island, Kochi, serve exhibitions, business conferences, corporate events, concerts and weddings.

Hotels & Resorts

Hotels, resorts and integrated hospitality developments that complement the group’s convention, tourism and commercial destinations.

Logistics Hub

Warehousing, industrial support and distribution infrastructure that serves regional trade. The executive profile lists Whirlpool of India and MyG India as commercial tenants in Kakkancheri, near KINFRA Industrial Park in Malappuram.

Retail & Commercial Destinations

IGNIVA Calicut Beach Bay brings shopping, restaurants, cafés and commercial services together in Calicut. The executive profile lists Starbucks, Paragon, Breklers and Solus among its tenants and commercial partners.

Technology Retail

IGNIVA 4N Bazar Tech Mall brings together more than 100 shops focused on mobile phones, accessories and consumer electronics.

Leadership

Building destinations that bring business together.

Nijesh Puthalath established IGNIVA Group in 2015. His work focuses on developing commercial assets and integrated destinations for business, commerce, entertainment and everyday life.

Based in Kerala, the group spans convention and trade centres, hospitality, logistics, commercial destinations and technology retail. His approach combines long-term asset creation with strategic investment, commercial partnerships and professional management.

An MBA graduate from London, he brings experience in corporate banking, financial services, business development and investments. He is active in the startup ecosystems of India and the UAE, with interests in strategic partnerships, early-stage funding, hospitality and brand expansion.
