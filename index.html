04 — Our journey

From Kerala to anew chapter.

Under Nijesh Puthalath’s leadership, IGNIVA has grown into a group spanning trade, hospitality, retail, logistics and technology.

2015TradeGrowthNext
2015 · Kerala

IGNIVA Group begins

Drawing on experience in corporate banking, financial services and investment, Nijesh Puthalath founded IGNIVA to develop commercial assets for the long term — combining investment, infrastructure and partnerships with the businesses that use those spaces.

Convention & trade

Creating room for business to gather

Convention and exhibition venues became central to the portfolio. Calicut Trade Centre and Cochin Trade Centre bring trade, exhibitions and live events to two Kerala cities.

Hospitality, retail & logistics

Expanding into new sectors

IGNIVA Airport Hotel, Calicut Beach Bay and 4in Bazar Tech Mall extended the portfolio into hospitality and retail, while IGNIVA Logistics Hub added warehousing and distribution space.

Proposed · Willingdon Island

Looking ahead to Tech Island

Phase I proposes IT parks, coworking and a play area. Phase II proposes a dedicated data centre — extending IGNIVA into technology and digital infrastructure.
